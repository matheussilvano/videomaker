# Code of Conduct

We follow the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/): be respectful, assume good faith, no harassment or discrimination, keep discussions technical and kind, and never share other people's personal or academic data. Maintainers may remove content or contributors that violate this. Report problems through a private GitHub security advisory contact or by opening a minimal issue asking for maintainer contact.
